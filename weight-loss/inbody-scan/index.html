What Is an InBody Scan?

An InBody scan is a body composition analysis that uses bioelectrical impedance, a safe, very low-level electrical current that travels through your body. Because muscle, fat, and water conduct electricity differently, the InBody analyzer can estimate how much of each your body contains, and where it’s distributed. You simply stand barefoot on the platform and hold the handles. There’s no radiation and nothing to feel.

What the InBody Scan Measures

Skeletal muscle mass

The muscle that powers movement and metabolism

Body fat mass and body fat percentage

How much of your weight is fat

Visceral fat level

The deep belly fat around your organs linked to diabetes and heart disease

Total body water

Hydration and fluid balance

Segmental analysis

Muscle and fat in each arm, leg, and your trunk, highlighting imbalances

Basal metabolic rate (BMR)

An estimate of the calories your body burns at rest

Why Body Composition Matters More Than the Scale

Two people can weigh the same and have very different health. A scale and BMI can’t tell fat from muscle, so they can miss what matters most:

  • Losing fat, not muscle: fast weight loss, especially on GLP-1 medications, can take muscle with it. InBody helps catch that early.
  • Hidden visceral fat: someone with a “normal” weight can still carry risky belly fat.
  • Progress when the scale stalls: building muscle while losing fat can keep your weight flat even as your body changes.
  • A more accurate starting point: your plan is built on your actual composition, not an estimate.

Who Can Benefit from an InBody Scan?

  • Weight loss patients: track fat loss and protect muscle
  • Patients on semaglutide or tirzepatide: make sure weight loss is coming from fat
  • People managing chronic conditions: monitor visceral fat linked to diabetes and heart disease
  • Adults over 50: check for age-related muscle loss (sarcopenia)
  • Athletes and active adults: measure muscle gains and training progress
  • Anyone starting a health plan: get an accurate baseline

How to Prepare for Your InBody Scan

For the most accurate and consistent results:

  • Avoid eating for 2–3 hours before your scan
  • Skip exercise the morning of your scan
  • Use the restroom before testing
  • Avoid caffeine and alcohol for several hours beforehand
  • Wear light clothing and remove shoes, socks, and metal jewelry
  • Try to scan at the same time of day for repeat tests

Not recommended for: people with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic medical device, or during pregnancy. Let us know if either applies to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get an InBody scan in San Antonio, TX?

Alamo Primary Care offers InBody body composition scans at its Westover Hills clinic, 12047 Potranco Rd, San Antonio, TX 78253. The scan takes about 60 seconds and measures muscle, body fat, visceral fat, and body water, with results reviewed by a medical provider.

Where can I get a body composition test in Boerne, TX?

Alamo Primary Care provides InBody body composition testing at 1411 S Main Street, Boerne, TX 78006, open Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm. Results are printed immediately and explained by your provider as part of your health or weight loss plan.

What does an InBody scan measure?

An InBody scan measures skeletal muscle mass, body fat mass, body fat percentage, visceral fat level, total body water, segmental muscle and fat in each limb and the trunk, and basal metabolic rate. Alamo Primary Care uses these results to guide personalized care.

How accurate is an InBody scan?

InBody uses multi-frequency bioelectrical impedance and is considered reliable for tracking body composition changes over time, especially when scans are done under consistent conditions. DEXA scans are more precise but cost more and involve low-dose X-rays. InBody is ideal for regular progress tracking.

How do I prepare for an InBody scan?

For accurate results, avoid eating for two to three hours, skip exercise beforehand, use the restroom, avoid caffeine and alcohol, and remove shoes, socks, and metal jewelry. Alamo Primary Care recommends scanning at the same time of day for repeat tests.

Is an InBody scan safe?

Yes. An InBody scan uses a very low-level electrical current that you cannot feel, with no radiation. It is not recommended for people with pacemakers or other implanted electronic medical devices, or during pregnancy.

Why use InBody instead of a scale or BMI?

A scale and BMI cannot distinguish fat from muscle. An InBody scan shows whether weight changes come from fat, muscle, or water, and reveals visceral fat that a normal weight can hide. This helps Alamo Primary Care patients in San Antonio and Boerne lose fat while protecting muscle.

How often should I get an InBody scan?

Most patients at Alamo Primary Care repeat their InBody scan about every four weeks during a weight loss or fitness program. Monthly scans are frequent enough to show meaningful changes without being affected by daily fluctuations.

Should I get an InBody scan while on semaglutide or tirzepatide?

Yes. GLP-1 medications can cause rapid weight loss, and some of that loss may be muscle. Regular InBody scans at Alamo Primary Care help San Antonio and Boerne patients confirm they are losing fat and adjust nutrition or activity to protect muscle.
