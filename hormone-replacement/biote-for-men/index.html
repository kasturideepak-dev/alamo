Signs of Low Testosterone in Men

Testosterone declines gradually after about age 30, and for some men it drops low enough to affect daily life. Common signs include:

  • Low energy and afternoon crashes
  • Reduced sex drive or erectile changes
  • Loss of muscle and strength, even with regular training
  • Weight gain around the middle
  • Irritability, low mood, or less motivation
  • Poor focus and mental fog
  • Restless sleep and waking up unrefreshed

If several sound familiar, a morning blood test is the place to start.

How Biote Works for Men

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We review your symptoms, health history, medications, and goals, including whether you plan to have children, since testosterone therapy can affect fertility.

  2. 02

    Morning lab testing

    Blood work, usually drawn in the morning when testosterone is highest, measures total and free testosterone, estradiol, PSA, a complete blood count (including hematocrit), and other markers as needed.

  3. 03

    Lab review

    Your provider explains your results and recommends a pellet dose based on your levels, body composition, and symptoms.

  4. 04

    Pellet insertion

    A quick in-office procedure of about 10–15 minutes. After local numbing, several small pellets are placed under the skin of the upper hip through a tiny incision.

  5. 05

    Follow-up and monitoring

    Repeat labs about 4–6 weeks after your first insertion check testosterone, hematocrit, and PSA. Before each new pellet, about every 4–6 months, we review your symptoms and labs and adjust your dose.

Aftercare

  • Keep the site clean and dry, and leave the bandage on as instructed
  • Avoid baths, hot tubs, and swimming for a few days
  • Skip heavy lower-body workouts, running, and squats for about a week
  • Mild soreness or bruising is normal and usually fades within a week

What Results Can You Expect?

Every man responds differently, but many notice:

  • 012–4 weeks:better energy, mood, and sleep
  • 021–2 months:improved libido and focus
  • 033–6 months:gains in muscle and strength and reduced body fat, especially with regular exercise

Is Biote Safe for Men?

Testosterone pellet therapy is generally well tolerated when properly monitored, but it isn’t right for everyone. Biote pellets are compounded preparations and are not FDA-approved as finished products, though the testosterone they contain is used in FDA-approved medications. Because pellets can’t be removed once placed, we start with careful lab testing and conservative dosing. Biote may not be right for you if you:

  • Want to have children soon, since testosterone can lower sperm production and fertility
  • Have prostate or breast cancer
  • Have high red blood cell counts (polycythemia)
  • Have untreated severe sleep apnea or uncontrolled heart failure

Possible side effects include insertion-site soreness, acne or oily skin, fluid retention, higher red blood cell counts, and occasionally a pellet working its way out. We monitor hematocrit and PSA at every follow-up.

What are testosterone pellets?

Testosterone pellets are small implants, about the size of a grain of rice, placed under the skin of the upper hip. They release testosterone steadily over several months. Alamo Primary Care offers Biote testosterone pellets for men in San Antonio and Boerne.

How long do testosterone pellets last in men?

Biote testosterone pellets typically last about four to six months in men. Activity level, metabolism, stress, and dose can affect how long they last. Alamo Primary Care schedules new insertions based on each man’s symptoms and lab results.

Are testosterone pellets better than injections?

Neither is better for everyone. Pellets offer steady levels and no weekly injections, while injections allow easier dose changes and lower upfront cost. Alamo Primary Care offers both in San Antonio and Boerne and helps men choose based on labs, lifestyle, and preferences.

How soon will I feel results from testosterone pellets?

Many men notice improved energy, mood, and sleep within two to four weeks of their first pellet insertion. Libido and focus often improve within one to two months, while muscle and body composition changes usually take three to six months.

Does Biote testosterone affect fertility?

Yes. Testosterone therapy, including pellets, can lower sperm production and reduce fertility. Men who want to have children should discuss this before starting. Because pellets can’t be removed once placed, Alamo Primary Care reviews family planning goals before treatment.

Does testosterone pellet insertion hurt?

Most men feel only pressure. The upper hip is numbed with local anesthetic before several small pellets are placed through a tiny incision. The procedure takes about 10 to 15 minutes, and mild soreness typically fades within a week.

Can I work out after getting testosterone pellets?

Light activity like walking is usually fine right away, but men should avoid heavy lower-body workouts, running, and squats for about a week after insertion to help the pellets settle and prevent them from working out of the skin.

What are the side effects of testosterone pellets?

Possible side effects include soreness or bruising at the insertion site, acne or oily skin, fluid retention, higher red blood cell counts, and occasionally a pellet working its way out. Alamo Primary Care monitors hematocrit and PSA at every follow-up.

What labs are needed before Biote for men?

Men typically need morning blood tests for total and free testosterone, estradiol, PSA, and a complete blood count, including hematocrit. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne may add other tests based on symptoms and health history.
