TRT Program

TRT Program at Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio & Boerne

At Alamo Primary Care, we understand why testosterone matters to men. It does far more than drive libido: it helps maintain muscle and bone, supports red blood cell production, and plays a part in energy, mood, focus and how your body stores fat.

Levels fall gradually with age, and for some men they drop low enough to cause real symptoms that are easy to put down to getting older. Low testosterone is diagnosed with a blood test, and when it is confirmed alongside symptoms, treatment can make a meaningful difference.

Our TRT program is a membership: one monthly fee for a thorough first consultation, scheduled follow-ups with lab review, prescription management, injection training and a provider you can reach between visits, with treatment monitored closely for as long as you are on it.

Signs of Low Testosterone (Low T) in Men

If several of these sound familiar, a simple morning blood test is the place to start.

Low energy

Worn out by mid-afternoon, even on a good night's sleep.

Reduced sex drive

Less interest than before, or changes in erectile function.

Muscle loss

Strength slipping despite keeping up with training.

Weight gain

More fat around the middle that won't shift.

Poor sleep

Restless nights and waking up unrefreshed.

Mood & focus

Irritability, low mood or trouble concentrating.

How Testosterone Replacement Therapy Works

Most members receive testosterone injections, self-administered at home after one-on-one training. Your provider sets a starting dose based on your labs and symptoms, then adjusts it at follow-up visits to keep levels in a healthy range.

What we monitor at your 90-day visits:

  • Total and free testosterone
  • Hematocrit (red blood cell levels)
  • PSA (prostate health)
  • Estradiol, as needed
  • Blood pressure and overall symptoms

How Soon Does TRT Work?

Every man responds differently, but many notice changes in stages:

  • 013–6 weeks:improved energy, mood, and libido
  • 022–3 months:better sleep, focus, and erectile function for some men
  • 033–6 months:gains in muscle and strength, and reduced body fat, especially with exercise

Your 6-week and 90-day visits are when we check what’s changing and fine-tune your dose.

Who Is a Good Candidate for TRT?

TRT may be right for adult men who have symptoms of low testosterone and consistently low levels on morning blood tests. TRT may not be right for you if you:

  • Want to have children soon, since TRT can lower sperm production and fertility
  • Have untreated prostate or breast cancer
  • Have high red blood cell counts (polycythemia)
  • Have untreated severe sleep apnea or uncontrolled heart failure

How is low testosterone diagnosed?

Low testosterone is diagnosed with blood tests, usually taken in the morning when levels are highest, along with a review of symptoms and medical history. At Alamo Primary Care, a single lab result alone isn’t enough to start TRT.

What are the symptoms of low testosterone?

Common symptoms of low testosterone include fatigue, reduced sex drive, erectile changes, muscle loss, weight gain around the middle, poor sleep, irritability, and trouble concentrating. Men in San Antonio and Boerne can get tested at Alamo Primary Care with a morning blood test.

How long does it take for TRT to work?

Many men notice better energy, mood, and libido within three to six weeks of starting TRT. Changes in muscle, strength, and body fat usually take three to six months. Alamo Primary Care reviews progress at a 6-week follow-up and every 90 days.

Will I have to inject myself?

Most Alamo Primary Care TRT members self-inject testosterone at home. Each member receives one-on-one injection training until they feel comfortable, and can contact their provider with questions between visits.

Does TRT affect fertility?

Yes. Testosterone replacement therapy can lower sperm production and reduce fertility, sometimes significantly. Men who want to have children should discuss this with their provider before starting TRT. Alamo Primary Care reviews family planning goals before prescribing.

What are the side effects of TRT?

Possible TRT side effects include higher red blood cell counts, acne or oily skin, sleep changes, fluid retention, and reduced fertility. Alamo Primary Care monitors hematocrit, PSA, and testosterone levels every 90 days to manage side effects safely.

Is TRT safe?

TRT is generally considered safe for men with confirmed low testosterone when it is properly monitored with regular lab work. Alamo Primary Care checks testosterone, hematocrit, and PSA levels every 90 days and adjusts treatment as needed.

What is the difference between TRT injections and testosterone pellets?

TRT injections are self-administered at home, usually weekly or every two weeks, with doses easy to adjust. Testosterone pellets, such as Biote, are placed under the skin in the clinic and release hormone over four to six months. Alamo Primary Care offers both in San Antonio and Boerne.

Is TRT covered by insurance?

Insurance often covers lab tests and may cover testosterone medication when low testosterone is confirmed. The Alamo Primary Care TRT membership is a $150 monthly fee. Call 210-571-1338 to check what your plan covers.
