When to Go to the ER Instead

Acute care visits are for non-emergency concerns. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for:

  • chest pain
  • difficulty breathing
  • stroke symptoms (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech)
  • severe bleeding
  • serious head injury
  • severe allergic reactions with throat swelling
  • loss of consciousness
