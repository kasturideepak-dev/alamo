FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Will I look “done”?

Not if we can help it. We plan conservatively and would rather add a little at a follow-up than overdo it on the first visit.

Does it hurt?

Most people describe a brief pinch. Many fillers contain a numbing agent, and we can use topical numbing where it helps.

Is there downtime?

Usually very little. Some redness, swelling or bruising at the injection sites can happen and typically settles within days.

Can I combine treatments?

Often, yes. Botox, filler and Sculptra do different jobs, and your provider can plan them together or over several visits.

What happens at the first visit?

A consultation: we talk through your concerns, medical history and goals, then recommend a treatment or combination with clear pricing before anything is booked.
