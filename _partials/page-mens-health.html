Why Men’s Health Care Matters

Men are less likely than women to schedule routine check-ups and more likely to delay care for symptoms. Yet many leading health risks for men, including heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, and prostate and colorectal cancer, can be detected early and managed effectively with regular screenings. A trusted primary care doctor helps you stay ahead of these risks at every stage of life.

Men’s Health Services We Offer

Annual Physicals for Men

A comprehensive men’s physical includes a full exam, vital signs, weight and BMI, lab work, and a review of lifestyle, family history, and risk factors, giving you a clear picture of your health each year.

Prostate Health & Screening

We discuss prostate cancer screening, including PSA blood testing, based on your age and risk, and evaluate urinary symptoms such as frequent urination or weak flow that may signal an enlarged prostate (BPH).

Low Testosterone Evaluation

Fatigue, low energy, reduced sex drive, mood changes, and loss of muscle mass may be linked to low testosterone. Our physicians evaluate symptoms, order lab testing, and discuss treatment options when appropriate.

Erectile Dysfunction Treatment

Erectile dysfunction is common and often treatable, and it can be an early sign of heart disease or diabetes. We provide confidential evaluation, identify underlying causes, and recommend effective treatment.

Heart Health & Cardiovascular Screening

Blood pressure checks, cholesterol panels, EKGs, and risk assessments help detect heart disease early. We create personalized plans to lower your risk through lifestyle changes and medication when needed.

Diabetes & Metabolic Health

Screening for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes with A1C and glucose testing, plus ongoing management for men already living with diabetes.

Cancer Screenings

Guidance and referrals for colorectal cancer screening (starting at age 45), prostate cancer screening, skin cancer checks, and lung cancer screening for eligible current or former smokers.

Weight Management

Medically guided weight management plans that address nutrition, activity, and related conditions like high blood pressure and sleep apnea.

Mental Health & Stress

Stress, anxiety, depression, and sleep problems often go unaddressed in men. We offer a safe space to talk, screen for common conditions, and connect you with treatment or referrals.

  3. 03

    Men 50 and older

Your physician will personalize this schedule based on your health and family history.
