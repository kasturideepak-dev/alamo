What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable gels, most commonly made of hyaluronic acid (HA), a substance your skin already contains that attracts and holds moisture. As we age, the face naturally loses volume and structure. Fillers replace that lost volume, smooth lines, and add definition, with results visible immediately.

Most HA fillers also contain lidocaine for comfort, and they can be dissolved with an enzyme called hyaluronidase if needed.

Before and After Your Filler Treatment

Before your appointment:

  • Avoid aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil, vitamin E, and alcohol for a few days, if your provider approves, to reduce bruising
  • If you’ve had cold sores, tell your provider before lip filler; antiviral medication may be recommended
  • Avoid dental work and vaccines for about two weeks before and after, if possible
  • Plan around events, allowing at least two weeks for swelling to settle

After your treatment:

  • Apply a cool compress to reduce swelling
  • Avoid strenuous exercise for 24–48 hours
  • Avoid heat (saunas, hot yoga, hot tubs) for a few days
  • Don’t massage the area unless your provider instructs you to
  • For lip filler, avoid straws and kissing for the first day or two
  • Sleep with your head slightly elevated the first night

Are Dermal Fillers Safe?

Hyaluronic acid fillers are FDA-approved and widely used. Common, temporary side effects include swelling, bruising, tenderness, and redness. Rare but serious complications, such as filler blocking a blood vessel, can occur, which is why anatomy knowledge and an experienced medical provider matter. Because HA fillers can be dissolved, problems can be corrected quickly. Fillers aren’t recommended if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active skin infection, cold sore, or inflammation at the treatment area
  • Have a known allergy to hyaluronic acid or lidocaine
  • Have certain autoimmune conditions or bleeding disorders (discuss with your provider)

Dermal fillers are for adults 18 and older.

What is the difference between filler and Sculptra?
