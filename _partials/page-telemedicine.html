When an In-Person Visit Is Better

Telemedicine is convenient, but some care needs a hands-on exam. Annual physicals, vaccinations, lab work, and certain screenings are best done at our San Antonio or Boerne clinic. If your provider decides you need to be seen in person, we’ll help you schedule quickly.

For emergencies such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, or severe injury, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
