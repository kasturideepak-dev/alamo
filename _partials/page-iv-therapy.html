What Is IV Therapy?

IV (intravenous) therapy delivers fluids and nutrients through a small catheter placed in a vein. Because it bypasses the digestive system, your body can absorb nutrients more quickly and completely than with oral supplements. Most sessions take 30 to 60 minutes, and many patients feel the effects of rehydration the same day.
