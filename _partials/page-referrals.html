It depends on your insurance. Many HMO plans require a referral from your primary care physician before seeing a specialist, while PPO plans often do not. Alamo Primary Care can help patients in San Antonio and Boerne understand their plan’s referral requirements.

Yes. Alamo Primary Care stays connected with your specialist, receives updates and test results, and reviews findings with you. Patients in Boerne and San Antonio keep their primary care physician as the central point of contact for coordinated, continuous care.
