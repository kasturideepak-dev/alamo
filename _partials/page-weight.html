Prescription options

Tirzepatide and Semaglutide

Two once-weekly medications that work with your body's own appetite signals. Your provider will recommend one only if it is right for you.

Dual GIP + GLP-1 1× weekly

Tirzepatide

Acts on two gut hormone receptors, GIP and GLP-1, to reduce appetite, help you feel full sooner and improve how your body handles blood sugar.

How it's taken
A small injection under the skin, once a week
Dosing
Started low and increased gradually as you tolerate it
Often considered for
Adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition
vs
GLP-1 1× weekly

Semaglutide

Mimics GLP-1, a hormone released after eating, which slows digestion and quiets hunger so smaller portions feel satisfying.

How it's taken
A small injection under the skin, once a week
Dosing
Stepped up over several weeks to limit side effects
Often considered for
Adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition

Both medications need a prescription and a medical evaluation. Common side effects include nausea and digestive upset, especially early on. They are not suitable for everyone, including people with certain thyroid or pancreatic conditions.

InBody Weight Analyzer

See what you are losing, not just how much.

A scale cannot tell fat from muscle. Our InBody analyzer gives a comprehensive breakdown of your body composition in about a minute, so we can make sure the weight coming off is the weight you want gone.

  • Muscle, fat and water measured separately
  • Visceral fat level, the fat around your organs
  • Repeat scans show progress between visits
  • Quick, non-invasive, done standing on a platform

Questions

Before you book

Can't find your answer? Call us on 210-571-1338 and our team will help.

Do I need to be on medication to join the program?

No. Many patients start with nutrition and activity changes alone. Medication is one option your provider may discuss if it suits your health and goals.

How do I know if tirzepatide or semaglutide is right for me?

Your provider reviews your history, current medications and labs before recommending either. Some conditions and medications rule them out.

What does an InBody scan involve?

You stand barefoot on the platform and hold the handles for about a minute. It is painless and you get a printed breakdown of your body composition.

Is delivery really free?

Free delivery of medication is available. Our team will confirm whether it applies to your prescription and address when you are prescribed.
