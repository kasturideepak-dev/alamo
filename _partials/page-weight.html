What to expect

How our medical weight loss program works in San Antonio & Boerne.

From your first visit to long-term maintenance, your plan is run by one primary care team at Westover Hills or Boerne. No hand-offs, no anonymous online prescribers. Here’s what each stage looks like.

  1. Week 1 · About 45–60 minutes

    Initial consultation & labs

    We start with your full medical history, current medications, past weight loss attempts, and goals. Your provider orders a baseline blood panel (BMP, CBC, TSH, lipid panel, and A1c), with a full wellness panel added if needed, so your plan starts from your numbers, not guesswork. Your first InBody scan records your starting muscle, fat, and visceral fat.

    Bring: photo ID, insurance card, and a list of medications and supplements.

  2. Week 1–2 · Once labs are back

    Your personalized plan

    Your provider reviews your results with you and builds a plan around your health, schedule, and preferences. That may include nutrition and activity guidance, semaglutide or tirzepatide if you qualify, and weekly lipotropic and B12 injections. If medication is part of your plan, we show you how to give your first weekly injection.

  3. Weeks 1 onward · About 10 minutes

    Weekly visits

    Quick weekly check-ins for your lipotropic and B12 injections, plus a weight and side-effect check. Many patients say these short visits are what keep them on track.

  4. Every 4 weeks

    Monthly InBody scans

    A repeat InBody scan shows exactly what you’re losing, so we can confirm it’s fat and not muscle, and adjust protein, activity, or medication if needed.

  5. Every 4 weeks

    Monthly provider follow-ups

    Your provider reviews your progress, InBody results, and side effects, then fine-tunes your plan. If you’re on semaglutide or tirzepatide, this is when dose increases are decided, one step at a time.

  6. Once you reach your goal

    Maintenance & keeping it off

    Reaching your goal isn’t the end. We shift to a maintenance plan with less frequent visits, nutrition and strength habits that protect your results, and, if you’re on medication, a careful plan to stay at a maintenance dose or taper safely. Learn about weight management →

Do I need to be on medication to join the program?

No. Medication is one option your provider may discuss if it suits your health and goals.

How do I know if tirzepatide or semaglutide is right for me?

Your provider reviews your history, current medications and labs before recommending either. Some conditions and medications rule them out.

Do you take insurance?

We accept most insurance plans, and your copay and deductible apply as usual. Cash pay options are available too. Call either clinic and our team will tell you which option works best for you.

What does an InBody scan involve?

You stand barefoot on the platform and hold the handles for about a minute. It is painless and you get a printed breakdown of your body composition.
