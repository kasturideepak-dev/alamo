How the program works

Prescription options

Two once-weekly options.

Both work with your body’s own appetite signals. Your provider will recommend one only if it is right for you.

Type

Dual GIP + GLP-1

Acts on two gut hormone receptors to reduce appetite, help you feel full sooner and improve how your body handles blood sugar.

Taken

Once a week

A small injection under the skin.

Dosing

Started low

Increased gradually as you tolerate it.

For

Considered for

Adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition.

Type

GLP-1

Mimics a hormone released after eating, which slows digestion and quiets hunger so smaller portions feel satisfying.

Taken

Once a week

A small injection under the skin.

Dosing

Stepped up

Over several weeks to limit side effects.

For

Considered for

Adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition.

Both need a prescription and a medical evaluation. Common side effects include nausea and digestive upset, especially early on. Not suitable for everyone, including people with certain thyroid or pancreatic conditions.

Do I need to be on medication to join the program?

No. Medication is one option your provider may discuss if it suits your health and goals.

How do I know if tirzepatide or semaglutide is right for me?

Your provider reviews your history, current medications and labs before recommending either. Some conditions and medications rule them out.

Do you take insurance?

We accept most insurance plans, and your copay and deductible apply as usual. Cash pay options are available too. Call either clinic and our team will tell you which option works best for you.

What does an InBody scan involve?

You stand barefoot on the platform and hold the handles for about a minute. It is painless and you get a printed breakdown of your body composition.
