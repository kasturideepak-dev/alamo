Our In-Office Procedures

Wound Repair & Stitches in San Antonio & Boerne

Need stitches but want to avoid the ER? Our physicians provide laceration repair for minor cuts, cleaning the wound and closing it with sutures, staples, or skin adhesive to support healing and minimize scarring. We also offer suture and staple removal and follow-up wound checks.

Pap Tests & Women’s Health Screening

Routine pap smears screen for cervical cancer and abnormal cell changes. Our San Antonio and Boerne offices perform pap tests privately as part of your well-woman exam, with HPV testing when recommended.

Foreign Body Removal

Splinters, glass, metal fragments, fish hooks, and objects lodged in the ear or nose can lead to infection if left untreated. We remove them safely in-office, often with local numbing for comfort.

Cyst & Abscess Drainage

Painful cysts, boils, and skin abscesses are treated through a quick incision and drainage procedure under local anesthesia. Draining relieves pressure and pain and helps prevent infection from spreading. Same-day appointments are often available for urgent cases.

When to Visit the ER Instead

Our clinic is ideal for minor procedures, but some situations need emergency care. Call 911 or go to the nearest ER for:

  • deep wounds with bleeding that won’t stop
  • exposed bone or tendon
  • severe burns
  • signs of serious infection like high fever and rapidly spreading redness
