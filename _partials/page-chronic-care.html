Why Chronic Disease Management Matters

Chronic conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and heart disease are the leading causes of illness and healthcare costs in the United States. Without consistent care, they can lead to serious complications like heart attack, stroke, kidney disease, and vision loss.

  • Reduce complicationsEarly adjustments prevent conditions from getting worse
  • Lower hospitalizations and ER visitsRegular monitoring catches problems before they become emergencies
  • Reduce healthcare costsPrevention typically costs far less than treating advanced disease
  • Improve quality of lifeBetter symptom control means more energy and independence

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get chronic disease management in Boerne, TX?

What is chronic disease management?

Chronic disease management is ongoing medical care that helps people control long-term conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease. It includes regular check-ups, lab monitoring, medication management, and lifestyle support. Alamo Primary Care provides this care for patients in San Antonio and Boerne.

Which doctor manages diabetes in San Antonio?

Primary care physicians manage most cases of type 2 diabetes and prediabetes. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne provides A1C testing, medication management, and lifestyle guidance, and coordinates with endocrinologists for more complex diabetes cases.

How often should I see my doctor for a chronic condition?

Most people with a chronic condition should see their doctor every three to six months, depending on how well the condition is controlled. Patients at Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio may need more frequent visits when starting or changing treatment.

Can a primary care doctor manage high blood pressure?

Yes, primary care doctors manage most cases of high blood pressure. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne monitors blood pressure, adjusts medications, supports lifestyle changes, and refers patients to a cardiologist when advanced care is needed.

Can chronic disease management be done through telemedicine?

Yes, many chronic care follow-ups can be done through secure telemedicine visits, including medication reviews, lab result discussions, and progress check-ins. Patients in Boerne, San Antonio, and the Hill Country can combine virtual and in-office visits.

How does chronic disease management reduce hospital visits?

Regular monitoring helps detect warning signs early, such as rising blood sugar or blood pressure, so treatment can be adjusted before a crisis occurs. This proactive approach at Alamo Primary Care helps San Antonio and Boerne patients avoid complications, ER visits, and hospital stays.

Does Medicare cover chronic care management?

Medicare Part B covers chronic care management services for patients with two or more chronic conditions expected to last at least 12 months. Coverage details and costs vary, so contact Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio or Boerne to confirm your eligibility.
