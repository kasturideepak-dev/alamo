What Is Sculptra?

Sculptra Aesthetic is an injectable collagen stimulator made of poly-L-lactic acid (PLLA), a biocompatible material used in medicine for decades. Unlike traditional fillers that add volume right away, Sculptra works beneath the skin to stimulate your body’s own collagen production. Over the following weeks and months, new collagen gradually rebuilds structure and improves skin quality.

Sculptra is FDA-approved to treat smile lines and other facial wrinkles, as well as cheek wrinkles.

How Sculptra Works

  • InjectionSculptra is placed into the deeper layers of the skin
  • Collagen stimulationThe PLLA particles prompt your body to produce new collagen
  • Gradual rebuildOver 2–3 months, new collagen restores volume and firmness
  • Long-lasting resultsThe PLLA is naturally absorbed, while the collagen your body made remains

Sculptra Aftercare: The 5-5-5 Rule

Massaging the treated area helps distribute Sculptra evenly and reduces the risk of small bumps. Follow the 5-5-5 rule:

  • Massage for 5 minutes
  • 5 times a day
  • For 5 days after each treatment
  • Apply a cool compress for swelling during the first day
  • Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours
  • Skip facials, saunas, and very hot environments for a few days
  • Avoid heavy makeup on the treated area for the first day

Your provider will show you exactly how to massage before you leave.

Is Sculptra Safe?

Sculptra has been FDA-approved for facial use since 2009 and is well studied. Common, temporary side effects include swelling, tenderness, redness, and bruising. Less commonly, small bumps (nodules) can form under the skin; proper technique and following the 5-5-5 massage rule help lower this risk. Sculptra isn’t recommended if you:

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active skin infection or inflammation at the treatment area
  • Have a history of keloids or hypertrophic scarring
  • Have a known allergy to any ingredient in Sculptra

Because Sculptra can’t be dissolved like hyaluronic acid filler, choosing an experienced medical provider matters.
