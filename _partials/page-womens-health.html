Why Regular Women’s Health Visits Matter

Many conditions that affect women, including cervical and breast cancer, thyroid disorders, osteoporosis, and heart disease, can develop with few early symptoms. Routine check-ups and screenings allow your physician to detect changes early, address concerns before they become serious, and support your health goals at every age. Heart disease is the leading cause of death among women in the United States, making preventive care especially important.

Women’s Health Screenings by Age

  1. 01

    Ages 21–29

    Annual well-woman exam, pap test every 3 years, blood pressure checks, STI screening as recommended, and HPV vaccine through age 26.

  2. 02

    Ages 30–39

    Pap test with HPV testing every 5 years (or pap alone every 3 years), cholesterol screening, and diabetes screening if at risk.

  3. 03

    Ages 40–49

    All of the above, plus mammograms (starting at 40 under current guidelines) and more regular heart health screening.

  4. 04

    Ages 50–64

    Mammograms every 1–2 years, colorectal cancer screening from 45, menopause care, and bone health evaluation.

  5. 05

    Ages 65+

    Bone density screening, continued mammograms as recommended, Medicare Annual Wellness Visits, and senior vaccines.

Your physician will personalize this schedule based on your health and family history.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get a well-woman exam in Boerne, TX?

Alamo Primary Care offers well-woman exams in Boerne and San Antonio, Texas. Each exam includes a physical and breast exam, pap test when due, health history review, and personalized screening recommendations from a primary care physician.

Where can I find a women’s health doctor in San Antonio?

Alamo Primary Care provides women’s health services in San Antonio and Boerne, including well-woman exams, pap tests, birth control counseling, menopause care, thyroid and hormone testing, and preventive screenings in a private, comfortable setting.

Can a primary care doctor do a pap smear?

Yes, primary care doctors can perform pap smears and HPV testing as part of routine cervical cancer screening. Alamo Primary Care offers pap tests at its Boerne and San Antonio offices, often during a well-woman exam.

How often should women get a pap smear?

Women aged 21 to 29 should have a pap test every three years. Women aged 30 to 65 can have a pap test every three years, an HPV test every five years, or both together every five years. Your Alamo Primary Care physician in San Antonio or Boerne will recommend the right schedule.

When should women start getting mammograms?

Current U.S. guidelines recommend women at average risk begin mammograms at age 40 and continue every two years. Women with a family history or higher risk may need to start earlier. Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio provides mammogram referrals.

Can my primary care doctor help with menopause symptoms?

Yes, primary care doctors commonly treat menopause and perimenopause symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats, sleep problems, and mood changes. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne offers lifestyle guidance and treatment options, including hormone therapy when appropriate.

Can I get birth control from a primary care doctor?

Yes, primary care doctors can prescribe birth control pills, patches, and other contraceptives and help you choose the best option. Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio provides contraception counseling and referrals for IUDs or implants.

Is a well-woman exam covered by insurance?

Most insurance plans cover an annual well-woman exam at no cost to the patient under the Affordable Care Act, including many screenings such as pap tests. Coverage varies by plan, so contact our San Antonio or Boerne office to confirm.
