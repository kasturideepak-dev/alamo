Testosterone Replacement Therapy

At Alamo Primary Care, we understand why testosterone matters to men. It helps maintain muscle and bone, supports red blood cell production, and plays a part in energy, mood, focus and libido. Levels fall gradually with age, and for some men they drop low enough to cause real symptoms.

Low testosterone is confirmed with a blood test. When it is, our membership covers everything around your treatment for one monthly fee, with your therapy monitored closely for as long as you are on it.

Does the $150 TRT membership include medication?

No. The membership covers your visits, lab review, prescription management, injection training and provider access. The medication itself is a separate cost.

How is low testosterone diagnosed?

With blood tests, usually taken in the morning when levels are highest, alongside a review of your symptoms and history. A single number on its own isn’t enough to start treatment.

Is Biote only for women?

No. Biote is available for both men and women, and every plan starts with a hormone lab panel and a consultation with your provider.

Are there side effects?

Hormone therapy can affect things like red blood cell counts, skin and sleep. That’s why treatment includes regular follow-ups and lab monitoring.
