Hormone Health

Hormone care that starts with your labs

Energy that never quite comes back, sleep that doesn’t restore you, a shorter fuse, a lower drive. These are easy to put down to age or stress, but they are often hormonal, and they can be measured.

We start with a conversation and a lab panel, then talk through what the numbers mean for you. If treatment makes sense, we offer two routes: our Testosterone Replacement Therapy membership for men, and Biote bioidentical hormone therapy for men and women.

  • Regular follow-ups and repeat labs
  • Options for both men and women
