Weight Management vs. Weight Loss: What’s the Difference?

Weight loss is about reaching a lower weight. Weight management is about everything that keeps you there: how you eat, move, sleep, and handle stress, and how conditions like diabetes, thyroid problems, or hormone changes affect your body. Our program covers both, but puts the long game first.

Weight loss program: a medically supervised plan that may include semaglutide or tirzepatide.

Weight management program: a sustainable plan for nutrition, activity, habits, and metabolic health, with or without medication.

  • People with weight-related conditions like prediabetes, type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, fatty liver, or sleep apnea
  • Women navigating weight changes in perimenopause and menopause
  • Anyone who has lost weight before and regained it, and wants a different approach
