Why Health Screenings Matter

Many serious health conditions, including high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol, and certain cancers, develop quietly with few or no early symptoms. Regular screenings help catch these conditions before they become serious, when treatment is simpler and more effective.

Early detection means better outcomes, fewer complications, lower long-term costs, and the peace of mind that comes from knowing your numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get a health screening in San Antonio, TX?

Alamo Primary Care provides preventive health screenings in San Antonio and Boerne, Texas, including blood pressure checks, cholesterol panels, diabetes testing, and cancer screening guidance. Screenings are personalized by a primary care physician based on your age, history, and risk factors.

What health screenings should adults get every year?

Most adults should have a yearly blood pressure check, weight and BMI assessment, and a wellness exam. Cholesterol and diabetes screenings are typically recommended every one to three years depending on risk, while cancer screenings follow age-based guidelines set by your physician.

What is the difference between a screening test and a diagnostic test?

A screening test checks for disease in people without symptoms, such as a routine cholesterol panel. A diagnostic test is used when symptoms are present or a screening result is abnormal, to confirm or rule out a specific condition and guide treatment.

Do I need to fast before blood work?

Fasting for 8 to 12 hours is usually required for fasting glucose and some cholesterol tests. Water is generally allowed. Alamo Primary Care will tell San Antonio and Boerne patients ahead of time whether their specific tests require fasting.

At what age should I start colorectal cancer screening?

Adults at average risk should begin colorectal cancer screening at age 45, according to current U.S. guidelines. People with a family history or other risk factors may need to start earlier, so discuss your timeline with your primary care physician.

How long does it take to get lab results?

Most routine lab results are available within one to three business days. Rapid tests such as strep and flu provide results during the visit. Alamo Primary Care reviews results with patients in person or through a telemedicine follow-up.

Are preventive health screenings covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans cover preventive screenings at no cost to the patient under the Affordable Care Act, though coverage varies by plan and test. Contact our San Antonio or Boerne office to confirm coverage before your visit.

Can I get my lab results explained through telemedicine?

Yes, Alamo Primary Care offers telemedicine visits for reviewing lab and screening results. Patients in San Antonio, Boerne, and the surrounding area can discuss results and next steps with their physician from home.
