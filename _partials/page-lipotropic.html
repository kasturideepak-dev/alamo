What Are Lipotropic Injections?

Lipotropic injections, often called lipo shots or lipo B12 injections, are blends of nutrients involved in how the body processes and uses fat. Our lipotropic injection, Lipolean, typically contains:

  • Methionine: an essential amino acid that supports fat metabolism in the liver
  • Inositol: a vitamin-like compound involved in breaking down fats
  • Choline: a nutrient that helps transport fat out of the liver
  • B vitamins: support energy production and normal metabolism

Together, methionine, inositol, and choline are known as MIC, which is why these shots are also called MIC B12 injections.

What Are B12 Shots?

Vitamin B12 is essential for energy, red blood cell production, and nerve health. A B12 shot delivers it directly into the muscle, bypassing the digestive system, which makes it especially useful for people who don’t absorb B12 well from food or supplements.

Who May Benefit from B12 Shots?

You may be at higher risk of low B12 if you:

  • Take metformin for diabetes or prediabetes
  • Take acid-reducing medications long term
  • Are on a GLP-1 medication like semaglutide or tirzepatide and eating significantly less
  • Follow a vegetarian or vegan diet
  • Are over 50, when B12 absorption naturally declines
  • Have had weight loss surgery or digestive conditions

Your provider can check your B12 level as part of your lab panel.

How Lipo B12 Injections Fit Into Your Weight Loss Plan

Lipotropic and B12 injections work best as support, not a shortcut. Patients most often use them alongside:

  • Nutrition and activity changes from their weight management plan
  • Semaglutide or tirzepatide, when prescribed
  • Monthly follow-ups and InBody scans to track fat and muscle changes

Many patients say weekly injection visits help them stay accountable and motivated. Evidence for lipotropic injections on their own is limited, so we use them as one part of a complete, medically guided plan.

Possible Side Effects

Lipotropic and B12 injections are generally well tolerated. Possible side effects include:

  • Mild soreness, redness, or bruising at the injection site
  • Mild stomach upset
  • Rarely, allergic reactions

Tell your provider about allergies, medications, and conditions such as kidney disease before starting.

What is in a lipotropic injection?

Lipotropic injections typically combine methionine, inositol, and choline (MIC) with B vitamins such as B12. These nutrients are involved in fat metabolism and energy production. Alamo Primary Care’s lipotropic injection, Lipolean, is used as part of a medically supervised weight loss plan.

What is the difference between lipo B12 and regular B12 shots?

A regular B12 shot contains only vitamin B12, which supports energy and nerve health. A lipo B12 shot adds lipotropic nutrients like methionine, inositol, and choline that are involved in fat metabolism. Alamo Primary Care offers both in San Antonio and Boerne.

Do lipotropic injections help with weight loss?

Research on lipotropic injections alone is limited. They are best used as support alongside nutrition changes, physical activity, and, when appropriate, medication. Alamo Primary Care offers them within a physician-led program in San Antonio and Boerne rather than as a standalone treatment.

What are the benefits of B12 shots?

B12 shots can raise low B12 levels quickly, which may improve fatigue, low energy, and symptoms such as tingling or poor concentration linked to deficiency. They are especially helpful for people who absorb B12 poorly, including those taking metformin or over age 50.
