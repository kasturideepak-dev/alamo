How often should adults get a check-up?

Most adults should have a preventive check-up once a year. Regular annual visits at Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne help track changes in blood pressure, weight, and lab results, and keep screenings and vaccines up to date.

What is included in an annual physical?

An annual physical typically includes vital signs, weight and BMI, a physical exam, review of medical and family history, medication review, age-appropriate screenings, lab work if needed, and vaccine updates. Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio personalizes each exam to your health needs.

What is the difference between an annual physical and a Medicare Annual Wellness Visit?

An annual physical includes a hands-on physical exam and often lab work. A Medicare Annual Wellness Visit focuses on creating a personalized prevention plan, reviewing health risks and medications, and does not usually include a full physical exam. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne offers both.

Where can I get a flu shot in Boerne or San Antonio?

Alamo Primary Care offers flu shots and other recommended vaccines at its Boerne and San Antonio offices. Adults should get a flu vaccine every year, ideally in early fall, before flu season peaks.

Is preventive care covered by insurance?

Most insurance plans cover preventive services such as annual wellness visits, many screenings, and recommended vaccines at no cost to the patient under the Affordable Care Act. Coverage varies by plan, so contact our San Antonio or Boerne office to confirm.
