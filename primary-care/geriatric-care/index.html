Personalized Care for Healthy Aging

Aging brings changes that call for a thoughtful, whole-person approach. Our geriatric care goes beyond treating individual conditions to consider how health, medications, mobility, memory, and daily life fit together. Whether you’re an active retiree in Boerne or caring for an aging parent in San Antonio, we build a care plan around what matters most to you.

Our Geriatric Care Services

Medicare Annual Wellness Visits

A yearly visit to create a personalized prevention plan, review health risks, update screenings, and set goals for the year ahead.

Chronic Condition Management

Ongoing care for conditions common in older adults, including diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease, COPD, arthritis, and osteoporosis.

Medication Management

Many seniors take several medications. We review every prescription and supplement to reduce side effects, avoid harmful interactions, and simplify routines where possible.

Memory and Cognitive Assessments

Screening for memory loss, confusion, and early signs of dementia or Alzheimer’s disease, with guidance, treatment, and referrals for patients and families.

Fall Prevention and Mobility

Balance and strength assessments, home safety advice, and referrals to physical therapy to reduce fall risk and help seniors stay mobile.

Bone Health and Osteoporosis Care

Bone density screening referrals and treatment to prevent fractures and maintain strength.

Senior Immunizations

Flu, pneumonia, shingles, RSV, Tdap, and COVID-19 vaccines recommended for adults 65 and older.

Vision, Hearing, and Sensory Checks

Basic screening and referrals to help seniors stay safe, connected, and independent.

Mental Health and Emotional Well-Being

Support for depression, anxiety, loneliness, and grief, which are common but often overlooked in older adults.

Advance Care Planning

Guidance on advance directives, medical power of attorney, and end-of-life wishes, so your care reflects your values.

Caregiver and Family Support

We work with family members and caregivers to coordinate care, answer questions, and make sure everyone is on the same page.

Specialist Coordination

When specialized care is needed, we coordinate referrals to cardiologists, neurologists, orthopedists, and others, and stay involved in your care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find a geriatric doctor in Boerne, TX?

Alamo Primary Care provides geriatric care in Boerne and San Antonio, Texas, for older adults who need personalized, whole-person care. Services include Medicare wellness visits, chronic condition management, medication reviews, memory assessments, and fall prevention.

Where can I find senior primary care in San Antonio?

Alamo Primary Care offers senior primary care in San Antonio and Boerne, with physicians experienced in caring for older adults. Care plans are tailored to each patient’s health status, independence, and preferences, with family and caregiver involvement welcome.

What is geriatric care?

Geriatric care is medical care focused on the health needs of older adults, typically 65 and older. It addresses chronic conditions, medications, memory, mobility, fall risk, and overall quality of life. Alamo Primary Care provides geriatric care for seniors in San Antonio and Boerne.

At what age should someone see a geriatric care provider?

Most people benefit from geriatric-focused care around age 65, or earlier if they have multiple chronic conditions, take several medications, or experience memory or mobility changes. Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio tailors care to each person’s needs, not just their age.

Does Medicare cover geriatric care visits?

Medicare Part B covers many geriatric services, including the Annual Wellness Visit, many preventive screenings, and treatment for chronic conditions. Coverage varies by plan, so seniors in San Antonio and Boerne should contact Alamo Primary Care to confirm their benefits.

Can my doctor check for memory loss or dementia?

Yes, primary care doctors can perform cognitive screenings to check for memory loss and early signs of dementia. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne offers memory assessments and connects patients and families with treatment, resources, and specialist referrals when needed.

How can seniors reduce their risk of falls?

Seniors can reduce fall risk through regular exercise, balance training, medication reviews, vision checks, and home safety changes like grab bars and better lighting. Alamo Primary Care in Boerne and San Antonio offers fall risk assessments and personalized prevention plans.

Can family members attend geriatric care appointments?

Yes, family members and caregivers are welcome at appointments with the patient’s consent. Alamo Primary Care in San Antonio and Boerne works closely with families to coordinate care, share updates, and support caregivers.
